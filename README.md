### Things that Zenith can do

- Open websites
- Check the Weather
- Send you notfications
- Check the time
- Say Hello and Goodbye!
- And more to come!

### What is Planned

- Send notifications over: Telegram, Matrix, Email
- Open Desktop Apps
- Talk to Zenith
- Recognise objects and faces through your webcam
- Connect to Self Hosted Services, like RomM, Home Assistant, Tandoor, Jellyfin, and Navidrome
